# 🌟 My Favorite Series: Breaking Bad

## 🎬 Overview
**Breaking Bad** is an American television series created by *Vince Gilligan*.  
It follows **Walter White**, a high school chemistry teacher who turns to cooking methamphetamine after being diagnosed with cancer.

---

## 🧪 Main Characters
- **Walter White** (Bryan Cranston)
- **Jesse Pinkman** (Aaron Paul)
- **Saul Goodman** (Bob Odenkirk)
- **Hank Schrader** (Dean Norris)

---

## 💭 Favorite Quote
> "I am not in danger, Skyler. I am the danger."  
> — Walter White
